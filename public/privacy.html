Privacy Policy

FiFi Recipes for Fire TV (package cooking.fifi.firetvapp, “the app”). Last updated 11 October 2026.

The short version

We collect no data. No accounts, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, no tracking. The app asks only for Internet and network-state access.

What the app does on your device

  • Downloads recipe content (JSON and images) from fifi.cooking over HTTPS.
  • Stores one setting on the device: your chosen language. It never leaves the device and is removed when you delete the app.
  • Loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives the device's IP address.

What fifi.cooking sees

Like any website, the static hosting that serves the app and the recipe data (GitHub Pages, behind Cloudflare) may log standard request metadata (IP address, timestamp, requested file) for security and abuse prevention. We do not receive or keep these logs.

YouTube

Video thumbnails load from YouTube when you open a recipe's videos, and the YouTube player loads from youtube.com only when you choose a video. Google's privacy policy applies to the player.

Children

Kids mode is for families cooking together; the app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, request data seen by our hosts (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) is processed on the basis of legitimate interests in delivering and protecting the service (art. 6(1)(f)); settings kept on your device are strictly necessary for features you use. These providers are in the United States and rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or “share” personal information as those words are used in California law, and we make no automated decisions about people.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to know, access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you (in practice, only emails you send), withdraw consent, object to or restrict processing, and receive a copy, under laws including Canada's PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25, the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and other U.S. state laws, and Brazil's LGPD. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher age set by local law). If a child has emailed us, a parent or guardian can ask us to delete it.
